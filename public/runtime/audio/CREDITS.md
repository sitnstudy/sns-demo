# Tetris music

`korobeiniki.mp3` — Korobeiniki, arranged by Kyrgyzstan (traditional melody).

- Source and license: https://creazilla.com/media/audio/15514735/korobeiniki
- Download: https://cdn.creazilla.com/sounds/15514735/korobeiniki-sound.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 2026-09-16. Audio is bundled unchanged.

This is a recording of Kyrgyzstan's MIDI arrangement of the familiar Tetris
theme, not Nintendo's Game Boy soundtrack recording. It replaces the player's
short oscillator rendition and loops locally without an external music service.
The existing block effects, mute preference, and completion fades are retained.
